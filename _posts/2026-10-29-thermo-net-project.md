**THERMO-NET: Industrial Heat-Transfer Modelling for Net-Zero Product Design**

- **Funding:** Swedish Energy Agency, Impact Innovation Net Zero Industry 2026
- **Project budget:** approximately SEK 12 million
- **Project period:** 2027–2030
- **Partners:** Lund University (Himani Garg, project lead), RISE Research Institutes of Sweden, Siemens Energy, TRATON GROUP and Ericsson

Existing engineering heat-transfer models can carry large uncertainties when they are applied to new fuels, complex surfaces and increasingly compact technologies. In practice, this uncertainty often leads to conservative designs, which need more cooling, more material and more energy. Together, we'll improve heat-transfer and pressure-loss modelling for industrial product design, by combining high-fidelity computational fluid dynamics (CFD) with reduced-order modelling to develop accurate and practical models that can be used directly in industrial product development.

Three industrial applications anchor the research:

- **gas turbines** transitioning towards fossil-free fuels
- **hydrogen-powered heavy vehicles** and their thermal management
- **compact telecom equipment** and its cooling

There's a lot of exciting work ahead. I'm looking forward to working with the whole consortium and translating detailed CFD insights into practical tools for designing more efficient net-zero technologies, with the aim of reducing thermal-design uncertainty, cooling demand and material use, and supporting faster development of competitive net-zero technologies for Swedish industry.
