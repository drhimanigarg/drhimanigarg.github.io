On 13 October I was invited to speak at the workshop **Bridging Rheology and SAXS/SANS: Strengthening Theoretical Links Between Structure and Flow**, organised by the [RheoMAXESS theme](https://www.linxs.se/advanced-rheometry-for-neutron-and-x-ray-science) at LINXS, the Lund Institute of Advanced Neutron and X-ray Science. The meeting brought together researchers from rheology, SAXS/SANS, soft matter, statistical physics, fluid mechanics and materials modelling to discuss one of the central challenges of complex fluids: connecting microscopic structure with macroscopic flow.

I contributed to the first challenge session, *How can constitutive modelling and SWAXS be linked in polymer materials?*, with a talk entitled **"From Constitutive Models to Flow-Induced Structure: What Can Simulations Tell Us About Viscoelastic Flows"**. I presented our work on the rheology and CFD of non-Newtonian flows, including flows around obstacles, and discussed what high-fidelity simulations can reveal about how the polymer microstructure evolves in a flow. This evolving microstructure is exactly what scattering experiments can observe, which makes simulation and SAXS/SANS natural partners. It also connects closely to our research on <a href="{{ '/research/' | relative_url }}">elastic turbulence and viscoelastic flows</a>.

The session also included a talk by Dragana Arlov of Tetra Pak on how SAXS/SANS can help address rheology challenges in food processing, followed by a lively discussion. Later sessions turned to viscoelastic and biological flows, data analysis at large-scale facilities, and the theory-guided protocols and observables that should be established at the facilities.

It was a pleasure to exchange ideas with experimentalists, theorists and computational scientists who look at the same materials from very different angles, and I came away with several ideas for possible collaborations. Many thanks to the organisers at LINXS, in particular the RheoMAXESS team, for the invitation and for a well-run, stimulating day. You can find the full programme on the [workshop page](https://www.linxs.se/events/2026/10/13/bridging-rheology-and-saxs-sans).
