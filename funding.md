# Funding

My research is made possible by the foundations, agencies and industrial partners who have chosen to support it. Since 2025 I have been awarded four grants as principal investigator, spanning heat transfer modelling for industry, hydrogen technologies, advanced cooling, and the physics of marine biofouling — and I am a co-applicant of the AdTherM competence centre.
